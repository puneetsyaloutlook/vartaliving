Richmond birdwing

Ornithoptera richmondius
Vulnerable under Queensland law

The Richmond birdwing in green, black and orange thread, centred on a square cover.

[Price]
Status
Vulnerable under Queensland law. Source: Queensland Government. Checked 3 October 2026.
Range
Once found from Grafton in New South Wales to Maryborough in Queensland. Now in two areas: Caboolture to Cootharaba, and Wardell to Ormeau and Mount Tamborine.
Host plants
Two native vines. Pararistolochia praevenosa below 600 metres, and P. laheyana above.
Wingspan
About 16 cm in females and 13 cm in males.
